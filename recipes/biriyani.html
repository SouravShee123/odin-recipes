Chicken Biriyani

Home

Description

Chicken Biryani is an aromatic, flavorful Indian rice dish made with tender marinated chicken, fragrant basmati rice, and warm spices.

Slow-cooked to perfection, this classic dish is packed with rich flavors and is ideal for special occasions or family dinners.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala powder
  • 1/4 cup ghee or cooking oil

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, biryani masala, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Par-boil the basmati rice with whole spices until it is about 70% cooked, then drain.
  3. Fry the sliced onions in ghee until golden brown and crispy, then set half aside for garnishing.
  4. Layer the marinated chicken and partially cooked rice in a heavy pot, top with fried onions, seal with a lid, and cook on low heat for 25 minutes.
