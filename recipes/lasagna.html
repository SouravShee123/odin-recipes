Lasagna

Home

Description

This classic lasagna recipe features rich meat sauce layered with tender pasta sheets and melted cheeses.

It takes a bit of prep time, but the end result is a warm, hearty meal that feeds a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 package lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 jar marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles in salted water according to package instructions, then drain.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a pan, drain excess fat, and mix in the marinara sauce.
  3. Layer the bottom of a baking dish with sauce, noodles, ricotta, and mozzarella. Repeat for three layers.
  4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 45 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling.
