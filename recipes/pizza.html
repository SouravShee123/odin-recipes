Pepperoni Pizza

Description

A classic homemade pepperoni pizza featuring a crispy outer crust, rich tomato sauce, and gooey melted cheese.

Making pizza from scratch lets you customize every topping and brings the pizzeria experience right into your kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced pepperoni

Steps

  1. Mix yeast, warm water, and flour together, knead into a smooth dough, and let it rise for 1 hour.
  2. Roll the dough out onto a pan or baking sheet to form your desired crust thickness.
  3. Spread an even layer of pizza sauce over the dough, followed by mozzarella cheese and pepperoni slices.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven at 450°F (230°C) for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
